Who I am

I turn market intelligence into products, systems and teams that perform.

Seven years in managerial roles have taught me that good design is only half the work. The other half is clarity: turning a stakeholder's vision into objectives, a realistic plan and a team that knows exactly what to do next.

Trained at Central St. Martins and later in UI & UX design, I move comfortably between graphics, industrial design, interiors, photography and digital products — and I communicate fluently in English, Cantonese and Mandarin, with working German.
